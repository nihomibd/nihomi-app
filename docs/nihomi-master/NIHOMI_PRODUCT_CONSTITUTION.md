## 1. Product Identity, Purpose & Audience

### 1.1 Product Definition
**Nihomi.com (にほみ)** is a premium, Bengali-first Japanese language learning and Japan-readiness practice web platform and installable Progressive Web App (PWA).

### 1.2 Approved Brand Promise
> **“From Zero Japanese to Japan-Ready.”**  
> *(শুরু থেকে জাপানের কর্মক্ষেত্র ও বাস্তব জীবনের জন্য প্রস্তুত।)*

### 1.3 Target Audience
1. **Bangladeshi Learners Starting from Zero:** Native Bengali speakers with zero prior exposure to the Japanese writing system or pronunciation.
2. **JLPT Aspirants (N5 through N3):** Students and professionals preparing for Japanese Language Proficiency Tests (JLPT) and NAT-TEST certifications.
3. **SSW & Technical Intern Relocation Candidates:** Skilled workers, engineers, nurses, hospitality staff, and students relocating to Japan who require situational conversational competence (*Baito*, convenience stores, restaurants, transit, and workplace *Keigo*).
4. **Intermediate Returning Learners:** Individuals with fragmented Japanese knowledge seeking systematic gap diagnosis, recall practice, and speaking fluency.

### 1.4 Strict Legal & Operational Boundary
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Non-Negotiable Legal Disclaimer:**  
> Nihomi.com is exclusively an **educational language technology and skills training platform**.  
> **Nihomi is NOT an immigration agency, recruitment firm, visa sponsor, or legal consultancy.**  
> Nihomi **does not guarantee** visa issuance, employment placement, university admission, or immigration outcomes, and must NEVER imply such guarantees in any marketing, curriculum, or conversational output.  
> Learning outcomes depend inherently on the learner's personal commitment, disciplined practice, and official testing authority evaluations.

## 2. Canonical Companion Identity: Nomi Sensei

### 2.1 Approved Public Names & Tagline
- **English Public Name:** `Nomi Sensei`
- **Bengali Public Name:** `নমি সেন্সেই`
- **Approved Bengali Tagline:** `“বাংলায় তোমার জাপানি শেখার সঙ্গী।”`
- **Strict Naming Rule:** Zero usage of the word "AI" inside public customer-facing companion titles or buttons. The companion is introduced as a warm, knowledgeable personal teacher and guide, not an artificial machine.

### 2.2 Persona & Pedagogical Demeanor
- **Warm, Patient & Respectful:** Nomi Sensei speaks in natural, encouraging Bengali.
- **Never Shame:** Wrong answers, slow recall, and stroke errors are treated as natural milestones in learning. Feedback must be constructive, concise, and calm.
- **Micro-Explanation Pattern:** Every error triggers a 1-sentence explanation in Bengali $\rightarrow$ a simplified retry prompt $\rightarrow$ celebration of recovery.
- **Emotional Boundaries:** Nomi Sensei never claims to possess superhuman emotional perception or omniscient knowledge. Support needs are inferred strictly from observable learning interactions (quiz scores, retry counts, stroke accuracy).
- **Privacy Boundary:** No secret background audio recording, camera monitoring, or ambient tracking. All voice and drawing inputs require explicit user activation.

## 3. Non-Negotiable Pedagogy: The Cumulative Lattice

### 3.1 Strict Cumulative Learning Lattice Law
> [!CAUTION]
> **Core Pedagogical Law:**  
> A learner must **NEVER** encounter an untaught Japanese character, sound-symbol combination, vocabulary word, or grammar structure inside learner-facing instructional content unless the prerequisite has been explicitly unlocked in their knowledge state.

- **Stage 1 (`あ`):** Only the single character `あ`. Zero compound words (`あい` is strictly locked). Zero full sentences.
- **Stage 2 (`い`):** Unlocked set: $\{ あ, い \}$. First unlocked vocabulary word: `あい` (*Ai* — ভালোবাসা).
- **Stage 3 (`う`):** Unlocked set: $\{ あ, い, う \}$. Unlocks: `いう` (*Iu* — বলা), `あう` (*Au* — দেখা করা).
- **Stage 4 (`え`):** Unlocked set: $\{ あ, い, う, え \}$. Unlocks: `いえ` (*Ie* — বাড়ি), `うえ` (*Ue* — উপরে), `え` (*E* — ছবি).
- **Stage 5 (`お`):** Unlocked set: $\{ あ, い, う, え, お \}$. Unlocks: `あお` (*Ao* — নীল), `おおい` (*Ooi* — অনেক). Followed by the 5-Vowel Milestone Review Gate.
- **Sensei AI Guard:** Any dynamic content or practice prompt generated by Nomi Sensei must pass automated prerequisite validation before rendering on screen.

### 3.2 "NO EVIDENCE = NO DONE"
- A button click, navigation event, page view, or elapsed timer is **never** counted as proof of mastery.
- Wrong answers, skipped exercises, and invalid writing strokes **never** increase student progress metrics.
- Mastery is earned exclusively through demonstrated recall, correct stroke sequence execution, and verified quiz answers.

### 3.3 Data Persistence Truthfulness
- Learner progress state must explicitly distinguish:
  1. `local_unverified`: Cached in client browser local storage.
  2. `cloud_persisted`: Verified and committed to durable PostgreSQL storage.
- The UI must never display "সব সংরক্ষিত" (All Saved) if network sync is pending or failed.

## 6. Engineering & Operational Principles

1. **One-Task-At-A-Time Discipline:** `AUDIT → SELECT ONE HIGHEST-PRIORITY BLOCKER → PLAN → IMPLEMENT → TEST → DEBUG → SECURITY REVIEW → VERIFY → DOCUMENT → COMMIT → CLOSE THE TASK`.
2. **Stateless Server Architecture:** The backend must remain completely stateless. All session, user, and subscription data resides in PostgreSQL.
3. **Local-First & Edge Compatibility:** The client application must handle network drops gracefully with offline banners and local fallbacks. Zero HTTP 405 errors on static deployments.
4. **Token Cost Protection (`aiCostGuard`):** Every AI invocation must pass through authentication, user quota tracking, sliding-window rate limits, and concurrency locks.
5. **No Evidence = No Done:** Every completion claim must point to an executable test, a verified exit code, or an authenticated artifact.
