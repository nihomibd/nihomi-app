# 👑 NIHOMI CONSTITUTION
## Canonical Law of the Nihomi Learning Operating System
**Version 1.0.0 — Constitution-Locked**

---

### PREAMBLE: THE NIHOMI NORTH STAR
Nihomi is not a conventional Japanese language school or a generic AI chatbot wrapper.
Nihomi is the **Japan Companion**:
> *"You learn Japanese. Nihomi coordinates everything else."*

The overarching mission is to guide any motivated student from **ZERO JAPANESE → JAPAN READY 🇯🇵** through an unbroken, delightful, and psychologically sound learning experience. Every learner must experience total cognitive clarity:
> *"I always know where I am, and I always know what to do next."*

---

## THE 10 IMMUTABLE LAWS

### LAW 1: ZERO-DISTRACTION ZEN LEARNING CANVAS
The learning interface is a sanctuary of focus.
- Every learning viewport must feel like a premium, tranquil space (Neo-Tokyo obsidian/slate theme `#0a0a12`, crystal contrast, glowing accents).
- Cognitive noise, redundant toolbar buttons, and cold engineering diagnosis boxes are strictly forbidden on the learning canvas.
- Language of instruction is natural, warm, contemporary Bangladeshi Bengali (ঢাকার সাবলীল মুখের ভাষা).

### LAW 2: STRICT CUMULATIVE LEARNING LATTICE
A learner must **NEVER** encounter an untaught Japanese character, sound-symbol combination, vocabulary word, or grammar rule inside learner-facing drills, quizzes, examples, or AI output unless all prerequisites have been explicitly mastered in their `LearnerKnowledgeState`.
- **Stage 1 (`あ`):** Learner only practices `あ`. Under no circumstances may `あい` appear.
- **Stage 2 (`い`):** After mastering `あ` and `い`, the first word unlocks: `あい` (Ai • ভালোবাসা).
- **Stage 3 (`う`):** Unlocks `いう`, `あう`.
- **Stage 4 (`え`):** Unlocks `いえ`, `うえ`, `え`.
- **Stage 5 (`お`):** Unlocks `あお`, `おおい`.
- **Milestone Gate:** 5-Vowel review gate must be passed before consonant families (`か〜ん`) unlock.
- **Grammar Prerequisite:** Grammar (Phase 10) is strictly locked until Hiragana, Katakana, and dual reading foundations are completely mastered.

### LAW 3: AUTHORITATIVE SINGLE SOURCE OF TRUTH
Curriculum progression, dashboard recommendations, lesson transitions, and completion CTAs are governed by one and only one engine: `getNextBestMission(learnerState)`.
- No isolated route-level redirections or hardcoded "next lesson" buttons may bypass the curriculum graph.
- Every system touchpoint reflects identical learner state and memory.

### LAW 4: VALUE-FIRST AUTHENTICATION AT "FIRST AHA"
Learners must experience genuine delight and tangible progress before being asked to sign up.
- Zero-Japanese learners start tracing immediately without auth barriers.
- The authentication prompt triggers naturally after the "First Aha" moment (`あ → い → あい` ❤️) with the warm message: *"Google দিয়ে আমার Journey Save করি"*.
- Progress made prior to login is seamlessly preserved across authentication events.

### LAW 5: DIGNIFIED MISTAKE RECOVERY
Mistakes are diagnostic stepping stones, never failures.
- No harsh red failure banners or shaming error alerts.
- Three-step repair cycle:
  1. Diagnostic detection of confusion type (visual, phonetic, recall delay).
  2. Compassionate micro-coaching in contemporary Bengali.
  3. Simplified retry leading to immediate confidence restoration and bonus XP.

### LAW 6: AI PROVIDER INDEPENDENCE & PEDAGOGICAL BOUNDARIES
Sensei AI is completely decoupled from any single AI vendor via the `SenseiProvider` interface.
- Core curriculum logic, lattice rules, and progression must never directly depend on third-party SDKs.
- All AI responses must pass through `assertSenseiOutputEligible` prior to rendering, ensuring no premature kanji or untaught grammar slip into student view.

### LAW 7: COMMERCIAL FAIRNESS & MONETIZATION PRESERVATION
Monetization is clean, honest, and respectful.
- Chapters 1 through 5 (Foundations through initial conversational building blocks) are 100% free.
- Chapter 6+ triggers a dignified Pro Preview Modal detailing what unlocks next without disrupting learner memory or corrupting state.
- Payment systems never silently lock students out of content they have earned.

### LAW 8: PLACEMENT RESPECT FOR EXPERIENCED LEARNERS
Learners who enter Nihomi already knowing Hiragana, Katakana, or elementary Japanese must not be forced to repeatedly trace `あ`.
- A 5-question diagnostic Placement Engine assesses vowel mastery, consonant recognition, katakana reading, and basic vocabulary.
- The engine computes their starting node in `CURRICULUM_GRAPH` and retroactively hydrates all prerequisite knowledge.

### LAW 9: JAPAN-READY HOLISTIC PREPARATION
Language is taught in direct context of Japanese daily life, career, and culture.
- From Day 1, learners discover how characters connect to Tokyo subway signs, Konbini shopping, visa interviews, and workplace communication.
- Cultural context and survival readiness are interwoven with JLPT milestones (N5 → N1).

### LAW 10: PRODUCTION INTEGRITY & AUTOMATED REGRESSION PROOF
No code, curriculum change, or design update may be deployed without:
1. 100% TypeScript type check (`tsc --noEmit`).
2. Clean production build (`npm run build`).
3. Automated unit and curriculum lattice validation.
4. Real headless browser verification with DOM evidence across all core learner personas.
