## 4. STRICT CUMULATIVE LEARNING LATTICE

- **Pedagogical Law:** A learner must **NEVER** encounter an untaught Japanese character, word, grammar pattern, or skill in learner-facing interactions unless the prerequisite has been explicitly taught and verified in their knowledge state.
  - Stage 1 (`あ`): Only single character `あ`. Zero compound words.
  - Stage 2 (`い`): Known set $\{ あ, い \}$. First compound word unlock: `あい` (Ai - ভালোবাসা).
  - Stage 3 (`う`): Known set $\{ あ, い, う \}$. Unlocks `いう`, `あう`.
  - Stage 4 (`え`): Known set $\{ あ, い, う, え \}$. Unlocks `いえ`, `うえ`, `え`.
  - Stage 5 (`お`): Known set $\{ あ, い, う, え, お \}$. Unlocks `あお`, `おおい`. Followed by 5-Vowel Milestone Review Gate.
- **“NO EVIDENCE = NO DONE”:** A click, passive scroll, or fake timer alone must **NEVER** award completion or mastery. Mastery requires verified answers, active character formation, or spoken evidence.
- **Mistake Recovery:** Diagnostic feedback must be delivered in warm Bengali explaining the visual or phonetic distinction. Recovery must be encouraging, non-punitive, and schedule an SRS review.
- **AI Sensei Grounding:** All generated Japanese output from Gemini AI Sensei must be validated against the learner's unlocked prerequisite graph before rendering.
