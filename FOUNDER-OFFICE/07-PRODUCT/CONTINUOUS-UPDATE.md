## 1. Evolutionary Feedback Loops
1. **Telemetry-Driven Iteration**: Every feature release is monitored for 7 days. If learner engagement fails to meet the target KPI, the feature is scheduled for refinement or deprecation.
2. **Weekly Curriculum Refresh**: Real-world Japanese workplace phrases, slang, and Tokyo situational contexts are infused into BaitoOS weekly.
3. **Continuous Performance Budgeting**: Total JavaScript bundle size must not exceed 1.6MB to ensure fast loading on mobile networks in Bangladesh.
