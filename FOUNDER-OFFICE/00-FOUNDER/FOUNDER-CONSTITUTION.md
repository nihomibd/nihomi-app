In the event of anomalous behavior, security breach, budget overflow, or hallucinated execution:

1. **Voice / Text Command**: The Founder issuing `"STOP ALL AI"`, `"EMERGENCY HALT"`, or activating the command center kill switch triggers immediate cessation of all background tasks.
2. **Immediate Freeze**:
   - All AI generation loops pause.
   - Outbound marketing emails and automated ads pause.
   - Database mutations from automated jobs revert to read-only.
3. **Post-Halt Investigation**: No system may be restored until an incident root-cause analysis is presented to and approved by the Founder.
