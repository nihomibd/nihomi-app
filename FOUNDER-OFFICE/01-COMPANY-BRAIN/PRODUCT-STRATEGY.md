# PRODUCT-STRATEGY.md — NIHOMI PLATFORM PRODUCT STRATEGY

---

## 1. Product Philosophy: "10X Futuristic Neo-Tokyo"
NIHOMI rejects boring, static, flashcard-only software. We immerse the learner in a vibrant Neo-Tokyo atmosphere (obsidian background `#0a0a12`, Hanabi particle effects, neon amber accents) that evokes the feeling of living in Tokyo from Day 1.

---

## 2. Core Feature Clusters

### A. The AI Pedagogy Suite
1. **AI Sensei & Vision Sensei**:
   - Conversational tutor powered by Gemini with context memory.
   - Vision Sensei analyzes handwritten kanji, street signage, and menu photos uploaded by students.
2. **Pitch Accent & Prosody DNA**:
   - Phoneme-by-phoneme voice feedback comparing learner pitch contour against native Tokyo pitch patterns.
3. **Adaptive Drill & SRS Engine**:
   - Spaced Repetition System tailored to learner forgetfulness curves for Kana, Kanji, and JLPT grammar points.

### B. Career & Relocation Modules
1. **BaitoOS (Workplace Survival Japanese)**:
   - Interactive roleplay scenarios: Convenience store (7-Eleven / Lawson cash registers), Izakaya service, hotel check-ins, and factory floor safety protocols.
2. **JLPT Mock Exam Engine**:
   - Timed, authentic JLPT N5–N3 simulations with automated scoring, diagnostic weak-point heatmaps, and instant explanations.
3. **Japan Twin / Cultural Simulation**:
   - Realistic life-navigation challenges (renting an apartment, sorting trash, registering at the ward office).

### C. Content Engine & Document Pipeline
1. **Automated Curriculum Ingestion**:
   - PDF ingestion, OCR parsing, structured lesson generation, automated vocabulary/kanji extraction, and one-click publishing via [ContentStudioView.tsx](file:///c:/NIHOMI/nihomi-app/src/views/ContentStudioView.tsx).
2. **Founder Approval Gate**:
   - Every published lesson must receive formal administrative or founder verification before being pushed to students.

---

## 3. Product Principles
- **Speed & Zero Lag**: Instant page transitions, service-worker asset caching, and optimistic UI updates.
- **Mobile First**: Fully responsive on low-end Android smartphones prevalent in emerging markets.
- **Offline Resilience**: PWA offline reading for downloaded drills and vocabulary cards.
