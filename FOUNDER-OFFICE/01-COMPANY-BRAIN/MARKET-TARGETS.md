## 1. Primary Market: Bangladesh to Japan Relocation Pipeline

### Demographic Profile
- **Age**: 18 – 32 years old.
- **Geography**: Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet, Rajshahi, Khulna, and emerging university towns.
- **Education**: HSC graduates, Polytechnic diploma holders, university undergrads (CSE, EEE, BBA), nursing and vocational students.
- **Key Pain Points**:
  - Outdated physical language coaching centers charging high upfront fees (৳25,000–৳50,000) with poor speaking practice and no AI feedback.
  - Fear of failing NAT-TEST / JLPT N5, which delays or invalidates visa applications.
  - Lack of practical conversational skills for Japanese part-time jobs (baito) and visa interviews.
  - Inconvenient payment options (absence of international credit cards; strong preference for bKash and Nagad).

## 2. Learner Archetypes & Personas

### Persona A: "The Engineering Aspirant" (Tech/SSW-1)
- **Goal**: Secure an IT / Software Engineering or Specified Skilled Worker (SSW) job in Tokyo.
- **Need**: Rapid JLPT N4/N3 certification + technical Japanese interview confidence.
- **Willingness to Pay**: High (Pro or Japan Ready tier).

### Persona B: "The University Study-in-Japan Seeker"
- **Goal**: MEXT scholarship or enrollment in a Japanese language school / undergraduate university.
- **Need**: High JLPT scores, Kanji mastery, SOP/Essay writing, Embassy interview coaching.
- **Willingness to Pay**: High (Japan Ready tier).

### Persona C: "The Vocational & Nursing Professional" (Kaigo / SSW)
- **Goal**: Nursing care or hospitality visa placement under Japan's revised immigration policy.
- **Need**: Spoken comprehension, workplace honorifics (Keigo), customer interaction simulations (BaitoOS).
- **Willingness to Pay**: Medium to High (Starter or Pro tier).

## 3. Secondary & Regional Markets
- **Diaspora in Japan**: Bangladeshi trainees and students currently in Japan needing rapid N3/N2 upskilling for visa changes.
- **South Asian Expansion (Phase 2)**: India, Nepal, Sri Lanka, and Vietnam facing identical Japanese language and labor export demand.
