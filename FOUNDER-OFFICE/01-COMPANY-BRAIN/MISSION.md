# MISSION.md — NIHOMI.COM CORPORATE MISSION

---

## 1. Primary Mission Statement
> **"Empowering 100,000 ambitious learners to master Japanese fluency, secure accredited career credentials, and successfully relocate to Japan with dignity, confidence, and financial independence."**

---

## 2. Our Commitments
1. **To the Learner**:
   - Deliver engaging, interactive, non-boring Japanese lessons powered by our futuristic Neo-Tokyo learning engine.
   - Provide instant phoneme-level pitch accent analysis and natural conversational roleplay simulations (BaitoOS).
   - Guarantee zero gatekeeping: free foundational tiers with transparent, flexible upgrade pathways.

2. **To Japanese Employers & Institutions**:
   - Provide thoroughly vetted, culturally fluent, and technologically proficient candidates who thrive immediately upon landing in Japan.
   - Ensure authentic certification verification (NAT-TEST / JLPT / JFT-Basic compliance).

3. **To the Business & Stakeholders**:
   - Build a sustainable, high-margin, cash-flow-positive subscription business (MRR-driven).
   - Maintain institutional frugality, continuous AI automation, and strict capital allocation under direct Founder oversight.
