## 1. Key Behavioral Patterns
1. **Device Footprint**:
   - ~78% Mobile traffic (primarily Android devices running Chrome/PWA).
   - ~22% Desktop traffic (used heavily for JLPT Mock Exams and comprehensive grammar sessions).
2. **Study Habits & Peak Activity**:
   - Primary study window: 8:00 PM – 12:30 AM BST (Bangladesh Standard Time), after university/work hours.
   - Secondary study window: 6:00 AM – 8:00 AM BST (early morning memorization).
3. **Primary Drop-off Points**:
   - Week 3 of Kanji memorization (cognitive fatigue).
   - Verb conjugation transitions (Te-form, Ta-form, Passive/Causative).
   - *Mitigation*: Automated AI cheerleading, bite-sized daily streaks, and coin reward incentives.
