## 1. Automated QA Checkpoints
1. **Furigana Sanity Check**: Verifies that every kanji has an accurate on'yomi or kun'yomi reading matching the specific sentence context.
2. **Bengali Clarity**: Checks that grammatical glosses use natural standard Bengali idioms rather than awkward machine translations.
3. **Audio Script Validation**: Verifies that Japanese TTS or voice prompts match the written sentence verbatim.
4. **Answer Key Determinism**: Guarantees that every multiple-choice question has exactly one unambiguously correct answer and three plausible distractors.
