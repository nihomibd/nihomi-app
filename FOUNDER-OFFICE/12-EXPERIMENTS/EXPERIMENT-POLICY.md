# EXPERIMENT-POLICY.md — SCIENTIFIC GROWTH & PRODUCT EXPERIMENTATION

**Scientific Lifecycle**: `GOAL → HYPOTHESIS → STRATEGIES → EXPERIMENT → MEASURE → COMPARE → REFINE → CONVERGENCE`  
**North Star**: Prioritize downstream business outcomes (MRR, JLPT pass rate, retention) over vanity metrics (page views, social likes).  

---

## 1. The 8-Phase Experimentation Pipeline

1. **GOAL**: Define an explicit commercial or educational outcome (e.g., increase bKash checkout completion by 15%).
2. **HYPOTHESIS**: Formulate a falsifiable, evidence-backed hypothesis conforming to [EXPERIMENT-SCHEMA.json](file:///c:/NIHOMI/nihomi-app/FOUNDER-OFFICE/12-EXPERIMENTS/EXPERIMENT-SCHEMA.json).
3. **STRATEGIES**: Design minimum of two distinct execution variants (Control vs Variant A/B).
4. **EXPERIMENT**: Run the test on a bounded sample size (max 20% of traffic or users) to mitigate business risk.
5. **MEASURE**: Collect statistically valid sample data without early stopping bias.
6. **COMPARE**: Perform statistical significance testing (p < 0.05).
7. **REFINE**: Iterate on winning elements; discard losing variants.
8. **CONVERGENCE**: Promote winning variant to default production status and archive learnings in Company Memory.
