## 2. Hard Budget Enforcement Rules
1. **Unilateral Freeze on Overage**:
   - If any wallet reaches its alert threshold (80%), AI Finance notifies AI COO and Founder.
   - If any wallet hits 100% of its monthly cap, all non-critical operations in that department halt automatically until reset or Founder approval.
2. **Zero Autonomous Re-allocation**:
   - Wallets cannot transfer surplus funds between each other autonomously.
   - Unused budget at month-end rolls into the Corporate Reserve, not into next month's departmental spending.
3. **No Financial Self-Expansion**:
   - Any prompt or script attempting to expand a wallet cap is blocked by the runtime safety guard.
