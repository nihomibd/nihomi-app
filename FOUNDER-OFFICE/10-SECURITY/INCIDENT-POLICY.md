# INCIDENT-POLICY.md — SECURITY INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY

---

## 1. 4-Phase Response Protocol
1. **Containment**: Immediate isolation of affected service, rotation of suspected compromised tokens, and activation of emergency maintenance page if required.
2. **Eradication**: Identification and elimination of root cause (patching code, banning malicious IP, tightening firewall).
3. **Recovery**: Verifying data integrity via database snapshots and testing endpoints in staging prior to production restoration.
4. **Post-Mortem & Disclosure**: Documentation of incident chronology, remediation steps, and notification of affected users if required by law.
