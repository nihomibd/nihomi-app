## 1. Thresholds Requiring Immediate Founder Escalation

1. **Financial Anomaly**: Any unexplained transaction discrepancy > ৳1,000 BDT.
2. **Infrastructure Failure**: Outage lasting > 10 consecutive minutes.
3. **Security Alert**: Any suspicious login attempt with admin privileges or unauthorized secret access.
4. **Legal / Regulatory Notice**: Any inbound communication from government ministries or educational boards.
5. **Brand Crisis**: High-profile negative public feedback or defamatory social campaigns.
